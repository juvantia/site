# juvantia.org

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors learning about Juvantia's physical proving ground, prospective citizens,
and operators choosing the official client for their device.

## Product Purpose

Explain Juvantia and connect visitors to its services and official downloads.
The site's role and approved design are recorded in `AGENTS.md` and
`docs/visual-direction.md`; `../style/AI_GUIDE.md` defines Neon Vitrum.

## Brand Personality

Physical, civic, signal-borne. Calm at rest, with light answering an interaction.
The land connects the interface to a real place.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboard cards, decorative robotics grids, neon fills, and speculative
content. Preserve the site's approved plate and glass-key components.

## Design Principles

- Keep the land and shell consistent across pages.
- Use the existing design language and components for small additions.
- Make choices explicit and explain differences in concise English.
- Link only to real, available destinations; unavailable downloads stay disabled.
- Preserve the approved content and scope each change to the requested task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and links, keyboard focus, readable contrast, and layouts
that fit phone, tablet and desktop widths. Respect reduced motion and keep
choices usable without hover. Interface copy is English; capitals come from CSS.
